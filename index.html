Breakfasts

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 1 small onion, very finely diced
  • 1 small tomato, finely diced
  • 1 green chili, minced
  • 1cm ginger, grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp cumin powder
  • ½ tsp Kashmiri chili
  • Small handful coriander, chopped
  • 2 tbsp ghee or butter
  • Splash of cream (optional)
  • Salt

Method

  1. Whisk eggs with salt and splash of cream. Set aside.
  2. Heat ghee on medium-low. Cook onion 5–6 min till soft. Add garlic and ginger, 1 min.
  3. Add turmeric, cumin, chili, stir 30 seconds. Add tomato, cook till it breaks down completely, 3–4 min.
  4. Turn heat to low. Pour in eggs. Stir gently and constantly with a wooden spoon. This is a pregnancy-safe akuri, so cook it gently until fully set with no wet patches. It will be firmer than traditional barely-set akuri.
  5. Off the heat once fully set. Coriander, green chili on top.

GD notes

Why it works

Spiced scrambled eggs — protein, fat, and aromatics. This is a pregnancy-safe version of akuri, cooked further than the traditional creamy version so the eggs are fully set.

Watch-out

Toast alongside is the only lever. The akuri itself is zero-carb.

Best GD serving

On their own, with cucumber salad or sliced tomato. If you want toast, one small slice of seeded sourdough.

If reading is high

Almost certainly outside the recipe — toast, sweet chai, juice.

Pregnancy notes
  • This pregnancy-safe version cooks the eggs fully through. No creamy/wet patches, no soft-set scramble.

Ingredients

  • Turkey slices
  • 2 eggs
  • Cheese
  • Hot sauce
  • Half a whole grain tortilla
  • Optional: peppers or onions, cooked through

Method

  1. Heat the turkey slices in a pan until hot. If they are deli-style slices, heat them properly rather than serving them cold.
  2. Cook the eggs until fully set and firm. No soft scramble, no runny yolk, no wet centre.
  3. Warm half a whole grain tortilla.
  4. Fill with eggs, turkey, cheese, hot sauce, and optional cooked peppers/onions.
  5. Fold tightly and serve immediately. No juice or extra starch on the side.

GD notes

Eggs, turkey, and cheese on a measured half-portion of wholegrain tortilla. Protein and fat anchor a controlled carb.

Watch-out

The tortilla is the carb. Half a wholegrain tortilla is the sensible portion — upgrading to a full one is what tips this over.

Best GD serving

As written: half a wholegrain tortilla, generous filling. No juice, hash browns, or refried beans on the side.

If reading is high

Drop to a quarter tortilla, or wrap the filling in a couple of large lettuce leaves instead. The filling is the meal.

  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 80g feta, crumbled
  • Big handful mint, chopped
  • 30g parmesan, grated
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp chili flakes
  • Salt, pepper

Method

  • 1cm ginger
  • ½ tsp cumin
  • Salt

Toppings

  • Diced tomato

Method

  1. Drain dal, blend with chili, ginger, cumin, salt, and just enough water to make a pancake batter consistency.

  4. Serve with the mint-yoghurt sauce.

Best GD serving

One or two chilla with the mint-yoghurt sauce, paneer, and salad topping. No chapati or toast on the side — the chilla is the carb.

If reading is high

Ingredients

  • 100g red lentils, rinsed
  • 500ml water
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1cm ginger, grated
  • 1 small green chili
  • Salt

Tarka (tempering)

  • 1 tbsp ghee
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 10 curry leaves (or skip)
  • 1 dried red chili
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced

To serve

  • Greek yoghurt
  • Coriander
  • Squeeze of lemon

Method

  1. Simmer lentils with water, turmeric, ginger, green chili, and salt for 20 min till completely broken down and porridge-like. Stir often, add water if needed.
  2. In a small pan, heat ghee. Splutter cumin and mustard seeds. Add curry leaves, dried chili, garlic. Sizzle 30 seconds till garlic is golden.
  3. Pour the tarka over the lentil porridge.
  4. Spoon into bowls, top with yoghurt, coriander, lemon.

GD notes

Why it works

Lentils slow-cooked to a porridge — pulses as the carb, releasing glucose gently. The ghee tarka adds fat which slows it further.

Watch-out

Bowl size. A small breakfast bowl is fine; a giant one adds up.

Best GD serving

A small bowl with the tarka and yoghurt. A poached egg on top for more protein. No bread on the side.

If reading is high

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 150g raw prawns, roughly chopped
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp lime juice
  • Pinch white pepper
  • 1 red chili, sliced
  • Coriander
  • 3 tbsp neutral oil (proper kai jiao is shallow-fried, that's where the texture comes from)

Method

  1. Whisk eggs vigorously with fish sauce, soy, lime, pepper until very frothy.
  2. Stir in prawns and spring onions.
  3. Heat oil in a wok or deep frying pan on high till shimmering.
  4. Pour eggs in from height (this is the secret — the splatter creates the crispy edges). They'll puff up immediately.
  5. Cook 2 min till underside is deeply golden, flip, 1–2 min more till prawns are fully pink and the omelette is set through.
  6. Drain on paper towel. Cut into wedges. Top with chili, coriander, lime.

GD notes

Why it works

Eggs and prawns shallow-fried — pure protein and fat, no carb. The technique gives the texture; the nutrition is the same as a baked omelette.

Watch-out

Almost nothing. Traditional serving is over rice — if you do that, the rice is the lever.

Best GD serving

Wedges on their own as a meal, or in lettuce cups with chili and lime. Skip the rice, or keep it to 3 tablespoons.

If reading is high

Rice portion if it was served alongside. Or a sweet chili dipping sauce.

Pregnancy notes
  • Method cooks the prawns until fully pink and opaque — follow it.
  • The deep-fry technique sets the eggs through fully.

Ingredients

  • 250ml unsweetened coconut milk
  • 100ml double cream
  • 1½ tsp powdered gelatine, or equivalent sheet gelatine
  • ½ tsp vanilla
  • Optional: tiny amount of sweetener if she uses one
  • Small handful raspberries
  • Squeeze of lemon

Method

  1. Warm coconut milk, cream, vanilla, and optional sweetener until steaming, not boiling.
  2. Bloom gelatine according to packet instructions, then whisk into the warm mixture until dissolved.
  3. Pour into 4 small ramekins and chill 4 hours until set.
  4. Crush raspberries with lemon and spoon a small amount on top.

GD notes

Why it works

Sweetness lever

Use unsweetened coconut milk. The raspberry spoon is the fruit portion; keep it small.

Best portion

One small ramekin.

After dinner when the meal itself was not carb-heavy.

If reading is borderline/high

Skip the raspberry spoon if borderline; skip the dessert if above target.

Ingredients

  • 200g almond flour
  • 250g pasteurised ricotta
  • 3 eggs
  • Zest of 1 orange
  • ½ tsp vanilla
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • Pinch salt
  • Optional: sweetener to taste
  • Butter for greasing

Method

  1. Heat oven to 170°C and grease a small cake tin.
  2. Whisk ricotta, eggs, orange zest, vanilla, salt, and optional sweetener.

  4. Bake 25–35 minutes until the centre is fully set and a skewer comes out clean.
  5. Cool completely before slicing.

GD notes

Why it works

Sweetness lever

Portion is still the lever. Almond flour cake is not free food.

Best portion

A small slice, ideally after a meal.

Best timing

Weekend dessert, not daily breakfast.

If reading is borderline/high

Ingredients

  • 300g full-fat Greek yoghurt
  • ½ tsp vanilla
  • Small handful chopped strawberries or blueberries
  • 2 tbsp chopped pistachios
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • Optional: tiny amount of sweetener if she uses one

Method

  1. Mix yoghurt, vanilla, chia, and optional sweetener.
  2. Spread thinly on a baking-paper-lined tray.
  3. Scatter with berries and pistachios.
  4. Freeze 2–3 hours, then break into pieces. Store frozen.

GD notes

Why it works

Greek yoghurt gives protein, chia and pistachios add fibre/fat, and berries are controlled by scattering rather than piling.

Sweetness lever

Do not turn it into frozen granola bark. No honey drizzle, no dried fruit, no chocolate chips unless tested.

Best portion

One or two small pieces.

Best timing

After a meal or as a controlled snack when readings are in range.

If reading is borderline/high

Skip it after an above-target reading. For borderline, keep to one small piece.

Pregnancy notes
  • Use pasteurised Greek yoghurt and wash berries well.
